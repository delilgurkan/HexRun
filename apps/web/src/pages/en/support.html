My loop says "under review"

If part of a run is far faster than running pace or the GPS jumped, the loop goes to review. Your run and streak are already saved; the map waits for the result, and we notify you when it's in. Use Add info on the run summary to give context (e.g. "went through a tunnel, GPS dropped"). To appeal a decision, email {{email.support}} with the date and time of the run.

My GPS trace broke or the loop didn't close

  • Allow location while using the app and keep precise location on.
  • On Android, battery saver can stop recording; turn off battery optimisation for HexRun.
  • Start outdoors, after the GPS signal has settled.
  • A loop closes when you get back within 50 m of your start point. The app guides you with a cue like "85 m to go".

A run from my watch didn't count on the map

  • Only runs that finished within the last 24 hours are applied to the map; older ones only go into your stats.
  • If the same run arrives from two sources (e.g. Garmin and Strava) it counts once.
  • Make sure your watch app has synced with your phone and the connection is still on under Profile › Settings.
