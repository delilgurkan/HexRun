Boilerplate

HexRun is a mobile app that turns running into a territory game. The city is a map of hexagonal cells; runners close a loop by returning to where they started and claim the cells inside it. Rivals take ground through duels and owners defend it by running. Routes are visible only to their owner and a privacy zone protects the area around home. Works with Garmin, Coros, Suunto, Polar, Apple Watch, Wear OS and Strava. For iOS and Android, in Turkish and English.

Usage

  • The wordmark is always lowercase: hexrun. In running text, write the name as HexRun.
  • Don't recolour, rotate, add shadows or gradients to the icon. Leave clear space of at least a quarter of the icon's height around it.
  • Use the logo only on paper (#F7F6F1), white or ink (#141716) backgrounds.
  • In Turkish the core move is called "halka" (loop) and cells are "petek".

Colours

The interface is only paper and ink; colour belongs to players. The eight player colours derive from the colour-blind-safe Okabe–Ito set. Amber is the brand's signature colour.
