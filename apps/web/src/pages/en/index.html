How it works

Three moves. All of them while running.

Everything happens through loops. A trace alone never changes the map; you have to get back to your start.

  1. {{step.run}} 01

    Run

    Open the app and start a run. Your phone or watch records the trace; your start point is marked on the map with a triangle.

  2. {{step.close}} 02

    Close the loop

    Come back within 50 metres of where you started. The loop closes there, and every return in the same run is a new loop. A cue like "85 m to go" guides you home.

  3. {{step.claim}} 03

    Claim

    Empty cells inside the loop become yours and your own cells get stronger. A rival's ground is taken through a duel.

Rules at a glance

An owner who runs as much as the rival never loses.

Power is counted per cell, duels per area. The rule set is short and the same for everyone.

  • Cells and power

    The city is split into fixed hexagons of about 300 m² each. Every cell has one owner and a power of 0–100. An empty cell becomes yours at power 10; a loop around your own cells adds +10 (up to 2 loops a day count).

  • Duels

    Pick 7–60 of a rival's cells and start a duel. Every loop around that area takes −10 off the duel's life, with up to 2 loops a day counting per duel. At zero the whole area is yours. The owner defends by looping it: life +20.

  • Decay

    If the owner skips loops for 24 hours, the cell loses 5 power a day. If an attacker stays away for 48 hours, their progress drops by 10 a day. A reminder, not a punishment.

  • Rookie period

    For your first 14 days, 3 loops a day count towards the same duel and short loops count too. That is how you make room in a crowded neighbourhood.

Events: each multiplier boosts a different move

  • Daily · 06:00–09:00 Morning Advantage 2x Power gained by owners on their own cells
  • Saturday–Sunday Weekend Blitz 2x Attack in duels
  • Daily · 18:00–21:00 Evening Defence 1.5x Owners pushing attackers back

Multipliers never stack and apply wherever you run. Claiming empty cells has no multiplier. For runs imported from a watch, the multiplier follows the time the run actually happened.

Fair play and privacy

Your route is yours. The map only shows cells.

In a running game, location is the most sensitive data there is. HexRun is designed around that.

  • Routes

    Only you see your loops

    Nobody else sees your route, start point or run times; they only see the colour and power of cells.

  • Privacy zone

    Around your home: "Hidden player"

    Choose a 200–800 m zone around your home. Cells inside it never show your name or initials; the centre is randomly offset per account and the circle is never drawn for others.

  • Retention

    Raw GPS for 30 days

    Raw location points are kept for 30 days, then reduced to a list of cells. You can export your data or delete your account from the app.

  • Anti-cheat

    Suspicious loops are held, not punished

    If the pace is far beyond running pace or the GPS jumps, the loop goes to review. Your run and streak are saved; the map waits for the result.

  • Sharing

    No map on the share card

    The card shows only the silhouette of your cells and the m². No streets, no route, no rival names, and cells in your privacy zone are left out.

  • Accessibility

    8 colour-blind-safe colours

    Neighbouring territories are never drawn in two colours that can be confused. Colour never carries meaning alone: outlines, initials and hatching do too.

Teams and leagues

Your rivals are your neighbours on the map.

  • League

    Starts in your neighbourhood

    Leagues start local, with individual and team rankings, weekly and monthly. Your own row is always visible.

  • Team

    Ranked by total m²

    Teams are ranked by their members' combined territory. Defence is individual: teammates can't help in a duel and there is no home turf.

  • Friends

    One reaction: applause

    The friends feed is visible to friends only and times are rounded. No comments; you see who is besieging you and who is running right now.

FAQ

Frequently asked

When exactly does a loop close?

When you come back within 50 metres of where the run started. Every return to the start in the same run is a new loop. If you never close one, your run and streak are still saved; only the map stays the same.

Can other people see my route or my home?

No. Loops and routes are visible only to their owner; other players only see the colour and power of cells. Cells inside your privacy zone say "Hidden player" instead of your name.

Can I play by bike or car?

No. HexRun is for walking and running. Stretches far beyond running pace and GPS jumps send a loop to review. See the terms of use.

Can I run with just my watch?

Yes. A run from Garmin, Coros, Suunto, Polar, Apple Watch, Wear OS or Strava counts on the map if it arrives within 24 hours of finishing.

How does a rival take my cells, and how do I defend?

A rival starts a duel over an area of yours and lowers its life with loops around it. Every loop you run around your own area adds +20 life and pushes the attacker back. An owner who runs as much as the rival never loses.

Can my team help me in a duel?

No. Duels are individual. Teams compete in the league with their members' combined territory.

Which cities can I play in?

The map is a fixed hex grid covering the whole world, so you can play wherever you run. Leagues start local, so your first rivals are in your neighbourhood.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the app: Profile › Settings › Delete account. If you can't access the app, use the email route on the account deletion page.
